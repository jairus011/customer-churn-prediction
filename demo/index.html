THE DATA SCIENCE LIFECYCLE

From a business question to a tested baseline

  1. Frame the problem

    Identify profiles associated with churn to support retention investigation. One row is one customer.

  2. Audit & explore

    Validate IDs, labels, missing values and numeric ranges. Explore training cohorts and distinguish counts from within-group churn rates.

  3. Prepare without leakage

    Use 17 predictors. Exclude Churn Label, Churn Reason, Churn Score, unverified CLTV and identifiers. Fit imputers, scaling and encoding within training folds.

  4. Train & select

    Stratified 60/20/20 split. Compare dummy, logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting through training-only cross-validation.

  5. Evaluate & explain

    Choose a threshold on validation F1, then evaluate the frozen model on the test set. Inspect error counts, subgroup metrics and validation importance.

  6. Deliver & monitor

    This browser demo runs the exported trained model. Production would require verified timestamps, a future horizon, temporal validation, calibration checks and outcome monitoring.

What this dataset can support

Historical churn classification and snapshot customer analysis. It cannot support monthly sales growth, causal retention claims, or validated next-month churn forecasts. The mentor’s banking PDF uses different data. Sensitive demographics are omitted from prediction, but proxy effects may remain.
